Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

These Terms & Conditions govern your access to and use of The Frontline Content Management System (CMS), operated by the official student publication of Gordon College, Olongapo City. By creating an account and using the system, you agree to be bound by these terms.

1. Eligibility

Access to The Frontline CMS is restricted to currently enrolled students, faculty advisers, and authorized staff of Gordon College. All users must register with a valid institutional email address. Accounts are non-transferable and may not be shared.

2. Account Responsibilities

You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials. Any activity performed under your account is your responsibility. You must notify the editorial board immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

3. Content Standards

All content submitted through the CMS must adhere to professional journalistic standards, including accuracy, fairness, and proper attribution. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Fabricated or plagiarized content
  • Defamatory, libelous, or malicious material
  • Content that violates the privacy rights of individuals
  • Material that promotes discrimination or hate speech
  • Unauthorized reproduction of copyrighted works

4. Editorial Oversight

The Editorial Board of The Frontline reserves the right to review, revise, approve, hold for further editing, or reject any content submitted through the CMS. Submission of an article does not guarantee publication. Editorial decisions are final.

5. Account Management

Editorial Board accounts require approval from a current administrator before access is granted. The Frontline reserves the right to suspend or permanently deactivate any account found in violation of these terms, without prior notice.

6. Accuracy of Information

By registering, you confirm that the personal information you have provided — including your name, email address, role, and section — is accurate and complete. Providing false information may result in immediate account termination.

7. Changes to These Terms

The Frontline may update these Terms & Conditions from time to time. Continued use of the CMS following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

For questions or concerns, contact us at thefrontline@gordoncollege.edu.ph.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Frontline, the official student publication of Gordon College, collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information submitted through its Content Management System (CMS).

1. Information We Collect

During registration and use of the CMS, we collect the following information:

  • Full name (first name and surname)
  • Institutional email address
  • Account role (Correspondent or Editorial Board)
  • Section assignment (for Correspondents)
  • Account creation date and status

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your CMS account
  • Facilitating the editorial workflow and content submission process
  • Communicating account-related updates, including approval notifications
  • Enforcing our Terms & Conditions and publication policies

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored securely using Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, services provided by Google Cloud. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to your account data is restricted to authorized administrators of The Frontline.

4. Session Data

The CMS uses browser session storage to keep you logged in while you are actively using the system. This data is cleared automatically when you close your browser session or log out. No persistent tracking cookies are used for advertising or analytics purposes.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account remains active or as required by Gordon College's institutional records policies. You may request the deletion of your account by contacting the editorial board directly.

6. Your Rights

As a user, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information held by The Frontline CMS. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7. Third-Party Services

The Frontline CMS uses Firebase (a Google service) for authentication and data storage. Your data may be subject to Google's privacy practices. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. We will notify users of significant changes through the CMS or by email. Continued use of the system after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

For privacy-related inquiries, contact us at thefrontline@gordoncollege.edu.ph.

Purpose

The Frontline aims to inform and empower the Gordon College community through student journalism and creative work. It provides a space for campus news, ideas, and perspectives to be shared with readers.

About the Publication

The Frontline is the official student publication of Gordon College. Its digital news portal features news, editorials, opinion, columns, features, literary works, development communication, sports, arts, and comics from its student contributors.

Contact Us

For any comments, suggestions, or inquiries, you may reach us at thefrontline@gordoncollege.edu.ph
